We have a system of two linear equations:

1.  x + y = 10
2.  x - y = 5

We can solve this using the **elimination method**, because the 'y' terms have opposite signs.

**Step 1: Add the two equations together.**

   (x + y) + (x - y) = 10 + 5
   x + y + x - y = 15
   2x + 0y = 15
   2x = 15

**Step 2: Solve for x.**

   2x = 15
   x = 15 / 2
   **x = 7.5**

**Step 3: Substitute the value of x back into either of the original equations to find y.**
Let's use the first equation (x + y = 10):

   7.5 + y = 10

**Step 4: Solve for y.**

   y = 10 - 7.5
   **y = 2.5**

**Solution:**
x = 7.5
y = 2.5

**Check the solution:**
*   For x + y = 10: 7.5 + 2.5 = 10 (Correct)
*   For x - y = 5: 7.5 - 2.5 = 5 (Correct)
While "murder" itself is quite specific (unlawful killing of a human with malice aforethought), there are several synonyms that carry similar meanings, some with additional nuances:

**Most Direct/General:**

*   **Homicide:** This is the legal term for any killing of a human by another human. Murder is a *type* of homicide. While broader, it's often used interchangeably in casual speech when an unlawful killing is implied.
*   **Slaying / Slay:** Often sounds a bit more archaic or literary, but directly means to kill, especially violently or intentionally.
*   **Killing:** This is the broadest term. To be a synonym for *murder*, it usually implies the unlawful and intentional aspect, or it's used in a context where "murder" is clearly the intended meaning (e.g., "The killing was premeditated").

**More Specific Types of Murder:**

*   **Assassination:** The murder of a prominent person, often for political or ideological reasons, usually by surprise or secret attack.
*   **Massacre:** The indiscriminate killing of many people, often with extreme brutality.
*   **Slaughter:** Similar to massacre, implying a brutal, indiscriminate killing, often of many.
*   **Butchery / Butcher:** Implies extreme brutality, often with disfigurement or dismemberment.
*   **Genocide:** The systematic killing of a large group of people, especially those of a particular ethnic group or nation.
*   **Extermination:** To kill off completely, often used in a dehumanizing way when applied to humans.

**Euphemistic or Understated Synonyms:**

*   **Liquidation:** Often used in organized crime or political contexts, meaning to kill someone.
*   **Elimination / Eliminate:** To get rid of someone, often by killing them.
*   **Dispatch / Dispatched:** To send someone to the other world; to kill, often quickly.
*   **Termination / Terminate:** A more clinical or euphemistic way to say kill.
